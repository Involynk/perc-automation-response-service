# PERC Hostel and Accommodation

## Does PERC Offer Hostel or Accommodation Facilities?

PERC — Personalized Education Research Centre — does **not currently offer hostel or accommodation facilities** on its campus. The institute is a day coaching center based in Begur, Bengaluru.

## Out-of-Scope Handling

If a student or parent inquires about hostel facilities, the recommended response is:

> "PERC is a day coaching center and does not have hostel or accommodation facilities. However, the Begur area in South Bengaluru has various private PG (paying guest) accommodations and rental options nearby. You can search on platforms like NoBroker, MagicBricks, or Google Maps for 'PG near Begur, Bengaluru'."

## Nearby Area Information

PERC is located in Visvapriya Layout, Begur, Bengaluru — a residential area in South Bengaluru. The area has:
- Good connectivity to Electronic City and the surrounding tech corridor
- Availability of private PG accommodations in the surrounding locality
- Proximity to residential neighborhoods
