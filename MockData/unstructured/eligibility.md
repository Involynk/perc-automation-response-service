# PERC Eligibility Criteria

## General Eligibility

PERC is open to motivated students seeking personalized academic coaching. Eligibility depends on the selected program and the student's current academic level.

PERC conducts an **initial counseling session followed by a diagnostic assessment test** to:

- Understand the student's current academic level
- Identify strengths and areas for improvement
- Recommend the most appropriate program and batch
- Understand the student's academic goals and preparation requirements

## Program-Wise Eligibility

| Program | Eligible Classes | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| CBSE Board | Classes 6-12 | Students studying under the CBSE curriculum |
| ICSE Board | Classes 6-10 | Students studying under the ICSE curriculum |
| JEE | Classes 11-12 and JEE aspirants | Students preparing for JEE Main and JEE Advanced |
| NEET | Classes 11-12 and NEET aspirants | Students preparing for NEET UG |

## Demo Class

Before making a final enrollment decision, students and parents are encouraged to attend a **free demo class**. This session allows:

- Students to experience the PERC teaching methodology firsthand
- Parents to interact with faculty
- Students and parents to understand the program structure
- Parents to discuss program details and fees

## Curriculum Board Compatibility

PERC currently offers dedicated programs for:

- CBSE
- ICSE

JEE and NEET preparation focuses on the respective competitive-exam syllabus along with the academic requirements relevant to the student's class.

## Diagnostic Assessment

The diagnostic assessment is used to understand the student's academic level and recommend the appropriate learning path. It is intended for evaluation and batch/program placement rather than as a competitive entrance examination.
