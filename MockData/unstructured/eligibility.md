# PERC Eligibility Criteria

## General Eligibility

PERC is open to all motivated students from Class 6 onwards. There is no entrance exam required for enrollment. Instead, PERC conducts a **diagnostic assessment test** after the initial counseling session to:
- Understand the student's current academic level
- Identify strengths and areas for improvement
- Allocate the student to the most appropriate batch and program

## Demo Class

Before making a final enrollment decision, students and parents are encouraged to attend a **free demo class**. This session allows:
- Students to experience PERC teaching methodology firsthand
- Parents to interact with faculty
- Both to ask questions about program details and fees

## Curriculum Board Compatibility

PERC supports students across multiple board curricula:
- CBSE
- ICSE
- NIOS (Class IX and X)
- State Board (for competitive exam aspirants)
