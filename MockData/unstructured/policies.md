# PERC Policies

## Academic Policies

### Batch Size Policy
PERC maintains a strict maximum batch size of **15 to 20 students** per batch to ensure:
- Individual attention for every student
- Effective doubt-clearing
- Personalized mentorship
- Better academic outcomes

### Testing and Assessment Policy
- **Weekly topic tests** are conducted after each topic is completed
- **Monthly comprehensive assessments** cover the full syllabus covered to date
- **Full-length mock tests** with All India ranking are conducted for JEE and NEET batches
- Test performance is shared with parents at monthly parent-teacher meetings

### Study Materials Policy
- All students receive **printed study materials** at the start of their program
- Additional worksheets and practice papers are provided throughout the course as topics are completed
- **Digital copies** of materials are also made available for reference

### Doubt-Clearing Policy
- **Dedicated doubt-clearing sessions** are conducted after every class
- Students can also schedule **one-on-one doubt sessions** with teachers as needed

### Parent Communication Policy
- **Monthly parent-teacher meetings** are held to discuss student progress
- **Progress reports** are shared regularly
- Parents receive **regular updates via phone and WhatsApp**
- Parents can schedule individual meetings with teachers at any time

## Admission Policies

### Rolling Admissions
PERC accepts new students throughout the year, subject to seat availability. Students should contact PERC early to secure a seat in their preferred batch.

### Demo Class Policy
PERC offers a **free demo class** to all prospective students and parents before enrollment, allowing them to experience the teaching methodology firsthand.

### Diagnostic Assessment
All new students undergo a **diagnostic assessment test** to determine their current academic level and the most appropriate batch placement.
