# PERC Scholarship and Merit Policy

PERC offers merit-based scholarships for high-performing students entering board and competitive exam preparation programs.

Scholarships are awarded based on performance in the diagnostic assessment test and previous academic year mark sheets.

Categories of Scholarships:
1. Academic Excellence Award: Up to 25% fee waiver for students scoring above 90% in school board examinations.
2. Competitive Exam Merit Waiver: Partial scholarship for top rankers in diagnostic assessments.

To apply for a scholarship, parents should present relevant mark sheets during the counseling session.
