# Out-of-Scope and Escalation Queries for PERC

This file contains examples of queries that fall outside PERC scope, along with the recommended handling approach.

---

## Category 1: Queries About Other Institutes

**User**: "Which is better, PERC or BYJU'S?"
**Handling**: Acknowledge the comparison request neutrally, highlight PERC key differentiators (small batches, personalized attention, local focus) without making negative claims about other institutes.
**Response approach**: "PERC focuses on small batches of 15-20 students, personalized study plans, and close parent communication. If you value individual attention over standardized mass coaching, PERC may be a great fit. Would you like to book a free demo to experience it firsthand?"

---

## Category 2: Questions About Other Schools / Colleges

**User**: "Which school should my child join in Bengaluru?"
**Handling**: Out of scope — PERC is a coaching institute, not a school.
**Response**: "PERC specializes in coaching and tuition for school students, not school admissions. For school recommendations, you may want to consult local education forums or education consultants in Bengaluru."

---

## Category 3: Career Counseling Beyond Academics

**User**: "My child wants to become a pilot. Does PERC help with that?"
**Handling**: Partially out of scope. PERC does not prepare students for pilot entrance tests specifically.
**Response**: "PERC specializes in JEE, NEET, KCET, CBSE, and ICSE coaching. For pilot training or NDA-specific coaching, you would need a specialized institute. However, we do offer strong Maths and Physics coaching which form the foundation for aviation aptitude tests."

---

## Category 4: Non-Academic / General Queries

**User**: "Can you recommend a good restaurant near Begur?"
**Handling**: Completely out of scope.
**Response**: "I can only assist with PERC-related queries — programs, admissions, fees, timings, and contact details. For local recommendations, Google Maps would be a great resource!"

## Category 5: Escalation to Human

When a query cannot be answered by the bot and requires direct human intervention:
- Fee negotiation requests
- Complaints about faculty or staff
- Refund disputes
- Special accommodation requests
- Grievances

**Escalation Response**: "This query needs direct attention from our team. Please contact PERC at +91 7259941873 or email perc.personalised@gmail.com and our staff will assist you."

---

## Category 6: Medical / Emergency Queries

**User**: "My child is having a panic attack before exams."
**Handling**: Immediate compassionate response + escalate to parent/guardian.
**Response**: "I am very sorry to hear that. Please ensure your child's safety first. For immediate support, contact a mental health helpline. For exam-related stress, PERC faculty are available to discuss strategies — please call +91 7259941873 to speak with our team."
