# PERC Branch and Location

## Main Campus

PERC currently operates a single main campus located in Begur, South Bengaluru.

### Address

**531, 20th Cross Rd, 1st Block, Visvapriya Layout,**
**Begur, Bengaluru, Karnataka 560114**

### Nearby Landmarks

- Visvapriya Nagar
- Begur Road
- Electronic City (nearby area)

### How to Reach

- The campus is in Visvapriya Layout, Begur, which is accessible from Begur Road and Electronic City Flyover.
- Easily reachable by auto-rickshaw, cab (Ola, Uber), or personal vehicle.
