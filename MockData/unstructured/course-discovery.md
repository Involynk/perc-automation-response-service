# PERC Course Discovery

PERC — Personalized Education Research Centre — offers courses across multiple academic categories. The institute is located in Begur, Bengaluru and provides personalized coaching for JEE, NEET, KCET, CBSE, and ICSE.

## Course Categories Available

- **PERC Core** — Foundation programs for Classes 6 through 10
- **NEET** — Medical entrance exam preparation
- **JEE** — Engineering entrance exam preparation
- **KCET** — Karnataka Common Entrance Test preparation
- **CBSE** — Central Board curriculum coaching
- **ICSE** — ICSE curriculum coaching
- **Foundation** — Olympiad and NTSE preparation
- **Personal** — One-to-One personalized tuition
