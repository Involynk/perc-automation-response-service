# PERC Course Discovery

PERC — Personalized Education Research Centre — offers personalized academic coaching across four key programs: CBSE Board, ICSE Board, JEE, and NEET. The institute is located in Begur, Bengaluru.

## Course Categories Available

- **CBSE Board** — Central Board curriculum coaching
- **ICSE Board** — ICSE curriculum coaching
- **JEE** — Engineering entrance exam preparation
- **NEET** — Medical entrance exam preparation

## All Programs at a Glance

| Program | Category | Class / Target | Duration |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| CBSE Board | Board | Classes 6-12 | 1 Year |
| ICSE Board | Board | Classes 6-10 | 1 Year |
| JEE | Competitive Exam | Classes 11-12 and JEE aspirants | 2 Years |
| NEET | Competitive Exam | Classes 11-12 and NEET aspirants | 2 Years |

## How to Discover the Right Course

Students and parents can:

1. Browse the available programs at perc.co.in/programs
2. Choose from CBSE Board, ICSE Board, JEE, or NEET
3. Book a free demo class or counseling call to get expert guidance on the right program
4. Complete the diagnostic assessment to help determine the appropriate learning path
