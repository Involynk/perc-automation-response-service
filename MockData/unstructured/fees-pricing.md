# PERC Fees and Pricing

## Fee Transparency Policy

PERC does not publicly publish exact fee amounts on its website. All fee details are shared transparently during the free counseling session or demo class. This allows the PERC team to discuss fees based on the specific program, batch timing, and student requirements.

## What Is Included in Fees

PERC fees typically cover:

- Printed study materials provided at the start of the program
- Additional worksheets and practice papers throughout the course
- Weekly topic tests
- Monthly comprehensive assessments
- Full-length mock tests with All India ranking for JEE and NEET competitive exam batches
- Dedicated doubt-clearing sessions after every class
- One-on-one doubt sessions with teachers on request
- Monthly parent-teacher meetings
- Regular progress reports
- Updates via phone and WhatsApp to parents

## Price Range

PERC does not publicly specify exact program fees. Current pricing should be confirmed directly with PERC during counseling or the demo session.
