# PERC Fees and Pricing

PERC does not publicly publish exact fee amounts on its website. All fee details are shared transparently during the free counseling session or demo class. This allows the PERC team to tailor fee discussions to the specific program, batch timing, and student requirements.

## What Is Included in Fees

PERC fees typically cover:

- Printed study materials provided at the start of the program
- Additional worksheets and practice papers throughout the course
- Weekly topic tests
- Monthly comprehensive assessments
- Full-length mock tests with All India ranking (for JEE / NEET competitive exam batches)
- Dedicated doubt-clearing sessions after every class
- One-on-one doubt sessions with teachers (on request)
- Monthly parent-teacher meetings
- Regular progress reports
- Updates via phone and WhatsApp to parents

## Price Range

PERC is positioned as a moderate-fee coaching institute (indicated as Rs Rs in the Google Business profile). It is not a budget coaching center, but is priced lower than large national coaching chains, with a strong focus on quality and personalization.
