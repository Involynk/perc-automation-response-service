# PERC Admission Process

## Overview

PERC follows a simple, transparent, and supportive admission process. The goal is to ensure every student is placed in the right program and batch before the enrollment is confirmed.

## Step-by-Step Admission Process

### Step 1: Book a Free Demo

Schedule a **free demo class** or a **free counseling call** to:
- Experience PERC teaching methodology firsthand
- Interact with faculty members
- Get answers to questions about programs and fees

How to book:
- Book via WhatsApp, phone call, or email with the PERC admissions team
- Schedule directly during a campus visit

---

### Step 2: Visit the Campus

Visit the PERC campus at:
**531, 20th Cross Rd, 1st Block, Visvapriya Layout, Begur, Bengaluru 560114**

During the visit, you can:
- Meet the faculty personally
- See the classroom facilities and academic environment
- Understand PERC approach to learning

---

### Step 3: Diagnostic Assessment

After the initial counseling session, the student appears for a **diagnostic assessment test** to:
- Assess current academic level
- Identify strengths and improvement areas
- Determine the best-fit batch and program

---

### Step 4: Choose the Right Program

Based on the diagnostic assessment and counseling:
- Counselors recommend the most suitable program
- Parents and students finalize the program and batch timing (B1: 4:30-6:30 PM or B2: 6:30-8:30 PM)

---

### Step 5: Complete Formalities and Start Learning

- Complete the enrollment formalities
- Receive printed study materials
- Begin classes with personalized attention and expert guidance

---

## Admissions Are Open Year-Round

PERC accepts enrollments on a rolling basis throughout the year, subject to seat availability. Each batch is limited to **15-20 students** to maintain quality and personalized attention.
