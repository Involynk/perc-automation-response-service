# PERC Course Details

Detailed information for each program offered at PERC — Personalized Education Research Centre, Begur, Bengaluru.

---

## CBSE Board

- **Target**: Classes 6-12
- **Category**: Board
- **Duration**: 1 Year
- **Subjects**: Mathematics, Science, English, Social Science
- **Focus**: Complete CBSE board preparation with strong conceptual understanding
- **Description**: The CBSE Board program provides structured coaching aligned with the CBSE curriculum. Students develop strong conceptual foundations through personalized teaching, regular assessments, doubt-clearing sessions, revision, and exam-oriented practice.

---

## ICSE Board

- **Target**: Classes 6-10
- **Category**: Board
- **Duration**: 1 Year
- **Subjects**: Mathematics, Science, English, History and Civics, Geography
- **Focus**: Complete ICSE curriculum preparation with strong subject fundamentals
- **Description**: The ICSE Board program provides specialized coaching aligned with the ICSE curriculum. Students receive concept-based teaching, regular assessments, intensive practice, doubt-clearing sessions, and structured preparation for ICSE examinations.

---

## JEE

- **Target**: Classes 11-12 and JEE aspirants
- **Category**: Competitive Exam
- **Duration**: 2 Years
- **Subjects**: Mathematics, Physics, Chemistry
- **Focus**: Complete preparation for JEE Main and JEE Advanced
- **Description**: The JEE program provides comprehensive preparation for JEE Main and JEE Advanced. It covers Mathematics, Physics, and Chemistry through conceptual learning, advanced problem-solving, regular tests, mock examinations, previous-year questions, and performance analysis.

---

## NEET

- **Target**: Classes 11-12 and NEET aspirants
- **Category**: Competitive Exam
- **Duration**: 2 Years
- **Subjects**: Physics, Chemistry, Biology
- **Focus**: Complete preparation for NEET UG
- **Description**: The NEET program provides comprehensive preparation for NEET UG with focused coverage of Physics, Chemistry, and Biology. The program includes concept-based learning, NCERT-focused preparation, regular tests, mock examinations, previous-year questions, and performance analysis.
