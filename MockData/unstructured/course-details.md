# PERC Course Details

Detailed information for each program offered at PERC — Personalized Education Research Centre, Begur, Bengaluru.

---

## PERC Ignite
- **Target**: Class 6
- **Category**: PERC Core
- **Duration**: 1 Year
- **Subjects**: Mathematics, Science
- **Focus**: Building strong foundations in Maths and Science
- **Description**: PERC Ignite is PERC first foundational program, designed for Class 6 students. It aims to build strong conceptual foundations in Maths and Science through personalized, structured, and engaging teaching. Small batch sizes ensure every student gets individual attention.

---

## PERC Explorer
- **Target**: Class 7
- **Category**: PERC Core
- **Duration**: 1 Year
- **Subjects**: Mathematics, Science
- **Focus**: Concept exploration and hands-on learning
- **Description**: PERC Explorer is designed for Class 7 students, focusing on deepening conceptual understanding through hands-on, exploratory learning methods. Students develop critical thinking skills alongside core academic subjects.

---

## PERC Challenger
- **Target**: Class 8
- **Category**: PERC Core
- **Duration**: 1 Year
- **Subjects**: Mathematics, Science
- **Focus**: Challenging problems and deeper understanding
- **Description**: PERC Challenger is for Class 8 students who are ready to tackle higher-level problems and develop deeper subject mastery. The program prepares students for the academic intensity of Classes 9 and 10.

---

## PERC Achiever
- **Target**: Class 9
- **Category**: PERC Core
- **Duration**: 1 Year
- **Subjects**: Mathematics, Science
- **Focus**: Board exam preparation and competitive edge
- **Description**: PERC Achiever prepares Class 9 students for board exams and builds a competitive academic edge for future entrance examinations. The curriculum bridges school board requirements and competitive exam thinking.

---

## PERC Champion
- **Target**: Class 10
- **Category**: PERC Core
- **Duration**: 1 Year
- **Subjects**: Mathematics, Science, English, Social Science
- **Focus**: Board exam mastery and confidence
- **Description**: PERC Champion is the flagship Class 10 program providing comprehensive board exam preparation with subject-wise mastery and confidence building. Students cover the full CBSE/ICSE syllabus with weekly tests and monthly assessments.

---

## NEET Foundation
- **Target**: Classes 9-10
- **Category**: NEET
- **Duration**: 1 Year
- **Subjects**: Physics, Chemistry, Biology
- **Focus**: Early NEET preparation
- **Description**: The NEET Foundation program helps Class 9-10 students begin structured, early preparation for NEET. It covers foundational PCB concepts aligned with both school board and NEET requirements.

---

## NEET UG
- **Target**: Classes 11-12 / NEET aspirants
- **Category**: NEET
- **Duration**: 2 Years
- **Subjects**: Physics, Chemistry, Biology
- **Focus**: Complete NEET preparation with Biology focus
- **Description**: The NEET UG program is a comprehensive 2-year course for students targeting NEET medical entrance. It covers the complete NEET syllabus with in-depth Biology, Physics, and Chemistry. Includes weekly tests, monthly assessments, and full-length mock tests with All India ranking.

---

## JEE Foundation
- **Target**: Classes 9-10
- **Category**: JEE
- **Duration**: 1 Year
- **Subjects**: Mathematics, Physics, Chemistry
- **Focus**: Early JEE preparation
- **Description**: The JEE Foundation program gives Classes 9-10 students an early head start for JEE Main. It covers core Mathematics, Physics, and Chemistry concepts aligned with both board and JEE requirements.

---

## IIT-JEE Advanced
- **Target**: JEE aspirants (Classes 11-12)
- **Category**: JEE
- **Duration**: 2 Years
- **Subjects**: Mathematics, Physics, Chemistry
- **Focus**: JEE Main and JEE Advanced preparation
- **Description**: The IIT-JEE Advanced program provides comprehensive 2-year preparation for JEE Main and JEE Advanced. The full syllabus is covered with advanced problem-solving, regular mock tests, and All India ranking assessments to benchmark student performance.

---

## KCET Crash Course
- **Target**: Class 12 / KCET aspirants
- **Category**: KCET
- **Duration**: 6 Months
- **Subjects**: Mathematics, Physics, Chemistry, Biology
- **Focus**: Intensive KCET preparation with mock tests
- **Description**: An intensive 6-month crash course specifically designed for Karnataka CET (KCET). Covers topic-wise revision, full-length mock tests, and previous year paper practice to maximize KCET scores for Karnataka government seat aspirants.

---

## CBSE Board Coaching
- **Target**: Classes 10-12
- **Category**: CBSE
- **Duration**: 1 Year
- **Subjects**: Mathematics, Science, English, Social Science
- **Focus**: Subject-wise coaching for CBSE Classes 10-12
- **Description**: Structured subject-wise coaching aligned with the CBSE curriculum for Classes 10 and 12. Includes regular tests, dedicated doubt-clearing sessions, and exam-oriented practice to maximize board scores.

---

## ICSE Board Coaching
- **Target**: Classes 9-10
- **Category**: ICSE
- **Duration**: 1 Year
- **Subjects**: Mathematics, Science, English, History and Civics, Geography
- **Focus**: Specialized coaching for ICSE curriculum
- **Description**: Specialized coaching tailored for the ICSE curriculum, covering all major subjects with intensive practice, topic-wise assessments, and detailed explanations suited to the ICSE exam pattern.

---

## Olympiad Foundation
- **Target**: Classes 6-10
- **Category**: Foundation
- **Duration**: 1 Year
- **Subjects**: Mathematics, Science
- **Focus**: NSO, IMO, NTSE preparation
- **Description**: The Olympiad Foundation program prepares students for national and international olympiad exams — NSO (Science Olympiad), IMO (Mathematics Olympiad) — and NTSE, through advanced problem-solving and analytical thinking.

---

## One-to-One Tuition
- **Target**: All classes
- **Category**: Personal
- **Duration**: Flexible (customized)
- **Subjects**: Mathematics, Science, English, or any subject
- **Focus**: Personalized private tuition
- **Description**: Fully individualized one-to-one private tuition with curriculum, schedule, and pace completely tailored to the student's needs and goals. Students can choose any subject or combination of subjects. The teacher works entirely around the student's learning style and available time.
