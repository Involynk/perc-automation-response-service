# PERC Placement and Career Outcomes

## About PERC and Career Outcomes

PERC — Personalized Education Research Centre — is a coaching institute focused on helping students excel in academic board exams and competitive entrance exams. PERC does not operate as a placement agency or college, so formal "placement records" do not apply in the traditional sense.

However, PERC tracks student outcomes in terms of board exam scores and entrance exam results.
