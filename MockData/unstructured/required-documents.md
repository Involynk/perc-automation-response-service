# Required Documents for PERC Admission

## Overview

PERC does not publicly list a formal documents checklist on its website. The documents required at the time of enrollment are discussed during the counseling session. The following are the commonly expected documents for admission to any coaching institute in India, and students should confirm the exact list with PERC directly.

## Commonly Required Documents

| Document | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Previous year mark sheet / report card | To assess academic level and batch allocation |
| School ID card or admission receipt | To verify current class of study |
| Aadhar card (student) | Identity proof |
| Passport-size photographs (2-4 copies) | For enrollment records |
| Parent or Guardian contact details | For communication and progress updates |

## For Competitive Exam Programs (JEE / NEET / KCET)

Additionally, for JEE, NEET, and KCET programs, students may be asked to provide:
- Class 10 mark sheet (if applying for Class 11-12 programs)
- Board affiliation information (CBSE / ICSE / State Board)
