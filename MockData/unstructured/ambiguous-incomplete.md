# Ambiguous and Incomplete Queries for PERC

This file contains examples of vague, ambiguous, or incomplete queries that a user might send, along with the recommended clarification approach and data needed to respond.

---

## Example 1: Vague Program Query

**User**: "What courses do you have?"
**Issue**: Too broad — does not specify exam, class, or goal.
**Recommended Response**: Ask clarifying question — "I can help! Could you tell me which class your child is in and whether you are looking for board exam coaching, JEE, NEET, KCET, or something else?"
**Data to use after clarification**: Course list in courses.json or course-discovery.md

---

## Example 2: Ambiguous "Best Course" Request

**User**: "Which is the best course at PERC?"
**Issue**: "Best" depends on the student's class, goal, and exam target.
**Recommended Response**: "The right course depends on your goal! Are you looking for board exam preparation, JEE/NEET/KCET coaching, or general foundation for Classes 6-10? Let me know and I will suggest the most suitable program."

---

## Example 3: Incomplete Location Query

**User**: "Where are you?"
**Issue**: Single-word or one-phrase location query.
**Recommended Response**: "PERC is located at 531, 20th Cross Rd, 1st Block, Visvapriya Layout, Begur, Bengaluru, Karnataka 560114. You can call +91 7259941873 for directions or reach out on WhatsApp: https://wa.me/917259941873"

## Example 4: Ambiguous Timing Query

**User**: "What are the timings?"
**Issue**: Could refer to batch timings, office hours, or demo class slots.
**Recommended Response**: "Sure! Here are PERC timings:
- Office hours: 10:00 AM - 8:00 PM, Monday to Saturday
- Batch B1: 4:30 PM - 6:30 PM
- Batch B2: 6:30 PM - 8:30 PM
Were you asking about a specific batch or program timing?"

---

## Example 5: Missing Grade / Exam Info

**User**: "I want to enroll my son."
**Issue**: No information about class, goal, or program preference.
**Recommended Response**: "Happy to help with enrollment! Could you share which class your son is in and what he is preparing for — board exams, JEE, NEET, or KCET? This will help me suggest the right program and next steps."

---

## Example 6: Typo / Misspelled Program Name

**User**: "Do you have IIT JEE Advance course?"
**Issue**: Minor typo — "Advance" instead of "Advanced"
**Recommended Response**: Assume the user means IIT-JEE Advanced. "Yes! PERC offers the IIT-JEE Advanced program — a comprehensive 2-year preparation course covering JEE Main and JEE Advanced. Batch size is 15-20 students. For fees and seat availability, please call +91 7259941873."
