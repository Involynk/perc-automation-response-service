# PERC Language and Medium of Instruction

## Primary Language of Instruction

PERC primarily conducts coaching in **English**. This is aligned with the CBSE, ICSE, and competitive exam (JEE, NEET, KCET) standards where English is the standard medium.

## Additional Languages Supported

Based on the structured data published on the PERC website (Google Business schema), PERC is available in:

| Language | Availability |
|---|---|
| English | Primary medium of instruction |
| Hindi | Available for communication and support |
| Kannada | Available for communication and support |

## Medium of Teaching

- **Classroom instruction**: Primarily in English
- **Doubt clearing**: Can be done in English, Hindi, or Kannada depending on the student's preference and faculty comfort
- **Study materials**: Printed materials are in English (aligned with board and exam standards)
- **Parent communication**: Can be conducted in English, Hindi, or Kannada

## For Kannada-Medium Students

PERC acknowledges that some students from Kannada-medium schools may join. Faculty are sensitive to language barriers and can provide additional support during doubt-clearing sessions in Kannada when needed.

## KCET and Karnataka Board Students

For KCET aspirants, the exam itself is conducted in English and Kannada. PERC KCET Crash Course is primarily taught in English with support available in Kannada.
