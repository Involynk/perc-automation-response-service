# PERC Availability and Status

## Current Admission Status

**Admissions: OPEN**

PERC accepts enrollments on a rolling basis throughout the year. New students can join at any time, subject to seat availability in their preferred batch.

## Batch Seat Limits

Each batch at PERC is strictly limited to **15 to 20 students** to ensure personalized attention. Seats fill up quickly, especially at the start of an academic year.

## Batch Timing Slots

| Slot | Time | Days Available |
|---|---|---|
| Batch 1 (B1) | 4:30 PM - 6:30 PM | Monday to Saturday |
| Batch 2 (B2) | 6:30 PM - 8:30 PM | Monday to Saturday |
| Weekend Batch | On inquiry | Saturday / Sunday (JEE and NEET batches only) |

## One-to-One Tuition Availability

One-to-One Tuition sessions are scheduled flexibly based on mutual availability of the student and assigned faculty member. Contact PERC to check faculty availability for specific subjects.
